# TALI-Core Overview

## Definition

TALI-Core is the shared wearable chassis and integration platform for TALI-H and TALI-W.

## Primary functions

- posterior thoraco-pelvic structural load transfer;
- adjustable and pressure-distributing human interface;
- module docking through the UTD;
- shared power and data routing;
- module identification and configuration management;
- independent safety supervision where required;
- sensor aggregation and logging;
- wearer control interface;
- system health and emergency state management.

## Structural principle

The core is thoracic in module origin but thoraco-pelvic in load transfer. High module loads must not be intentionally concentrated into unsupported shoulder, neck, clavicle, axillary, or rib soft-tissue regions.

For TALI-W, the full load path must be re-derived using aerodynamic, gust, manoeuvre, propulsion, deployment, landing, and failure load cases. The original auxiliary-limb structural assumptions are not sufficient for flight certification or substantiation.

## Human interface

The wearable interface should be adjustable, breathable, inspectable, and instrumentable. Candidate sensing includes interface pressure and retention/load monitoring. Human comfort is not itself structural proof; both pressure tolerance and rigid-body load paths require separate verification.

## Shared compute and safety

TALI-Core may host common mission/user-interface compute, but flight-critical TALI-W control may require segregated or redundant compute. The final partitioning will follow the safety assessment.
